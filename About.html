Our Story

Piquant Chilli Sauce was born from a passion for culinary excellence, agro-processing innovation and authentic South African flavour.

Founded by young South African entrepreneurs, our mission is to create handcrafted sauces that elevate every meal while contributing meaningfully to the country's growing agro-processing industry.

What started as a culinary vision has grown into an ambitious food brand dedicated to flavour, food safety, community impact and future innovation.
